# کانسپت‌های لوگو

روش تولید: ابزار داخلی imagegen. چهار کانسپت اولیه برای انتخاب؛ هیچ‌کدام لوگوی نهایی یا SVG تولیدی نیستند. نوشتار فارسی و جزئیات هندسی پس از انتخاب باید اصلاح و نهایی شوند.

مسیر تصاویر: assets/logo-concepts/concept-01.png تا concept-04.png. نام‌های طرح: 01 حرف D و کیف؛ 02 مونوگرام DS؛ 03 بسته دیجیتال؛ 04 کیف و نشانگر.

## Prompt 01

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE original logo concept for the Persian/English ecommerce brand Digishop, exact Persian name "دیجی شاپ", exact English name "Digishop". Concept 01: A distinctive geometric D monogram whose negative space subtly suggests an open shopping bag. Precise rounded geometry, premium and memorable, strong silhouette at favicon scale. Style: modern minimalist, flat vector-like shapes, professionally crafted optical spacing, no existing brand imitation. Color proposal: deep navy and a restrained teal accent on a clean warm-white background, monochrome legibility essential. Presentation: spacious square brand concept board showing one large icon centered above a neatly typeset bilingual wordmark, Persian "دیجی شاپ" and English "Digishop". Bottom area a small black monochrome version of the same icon to demonstrate scalability. Only this one design, no alternatives on the board. Exact text only those two brand names; no tagline, numbering, annotations or watermark. Crisp edges, no 3D mockups, no shadows, no glass texture; logo must work independently of glass UI.
