## پرامپت نهایی

Use case: logo-brand edit. Edit the referenced Digishop concept 02. Preserve its recognizable interlocking D and S ribbon monogram and deep navy/teal identity, but refine it into a more beautiful premium minimalist standalone symbol: balanced optical proportions, consistent rounded curves, clean negative spaces and fewer awkward intersections. Integrate a subtle shopping cart directly into the monogram geometry, for example its lower sweep becomes a cart base with two small balanced wheels and the D's upper-left stroke hints at a handle. The DS monogram must remain dominant and legible, the cart an elegant secondary reading, not an extra pasted icon. Remove ALL Persian and English wordmarks, all writing, and the small duplicate monochrome emblem at the bottom. Output ONLY ONE large centered finished logo symbol with generous transparent padding. Flat solid deep navy and teal shapes, crisp vector-like edges; no gradient, mockup, shadows, glow, texture, border or watermark. Genuine transparent background.
